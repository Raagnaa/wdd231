My Site Plan

Hunting website project for WDD 231

Site Name

Hunting Basics

I chose this name because the site is for beginners. I want the name to be easy to remember and tell people right away what the site is about.

If I bought a domain someday it could be something like huntingbasics.net

Site Purpose

My project is about hunting. The course asks for three pages total, so I will make a home page and two content pages. I will cover hunting techniques, safety rules, and equipment across those pages.

I also want to add some tips and pictures to help beginners understand better, because I am learning this topic too and I know it can feel confusing at first.

Pages I plan to make (3 total):

  • Home (index.html) - intro, links, and a few tips
  • Techniques (techniques.html) - basic methods and beginner tips
  • Safety and Equipment (safety.html) - safety rules and gear people usually use

Later in week 6 I will put the real site in its own folder like final or hunting inside my wdd231 repo.

Scenarios

These are questions someone might ask when they visit my site:

  1. What do I need to know about safety before I try hunting?
  2. What equipment should a beginner get first?

Color Scheme

I picked colors that look like the outdoors.

  • Dark green (#2d4a2e) - header, h2 titles, and wireframe borders
  • Tan (#f4f1ea) - page background
  • Brown (#8b4513) - links and wireframe boxes
  • Black (#1a1a1a) - normal paragraph text

Typography
