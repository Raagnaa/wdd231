Safety and Equipment

Safety comes first. This page has basic rules and a list of gear that beginners usually need.

Safety rules

  • Treat every gun like it is loaded.
  • Keep your finger off the trigger until you are ready to shoot.
  • Know what is behind your target.
  • Wear orange so other hunters can see you.
  • Follow the laws in your area.
